## Design Context

### Users
Mix of recruiters/hiring managers, fellow developers, and potential freelance clients. Portfolio needs to scan well quickly (recruiters) while showing personality and technical depth (devs) and trustworthiness (clients).

### Brand Personality
Warm, approachable, personal — like a friendly conversation. Not sterile or corporate. Zoe's personality should come through. 3 words: **warm, genuine, capable**.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Primary color**: Maroon `#420303` — warm, distinctive, not the typical tech-blue
- **Theme**: Both light and dark mode with toggle
- **Anti-references**: Generic AI portfolios (floating orbs, glassmorphism, gradient text, purple glows, card grids)
- **Direction**: Clean, warm, personal. Let the content and projects speak. Decoration should be minimal and intentional.

### Design Principles
1. **Authenticity over flash** — Every visual choice should feel deliberate, not decorative
2. **Content-first** — The projects, experience, and personality are the hero, not the UI effects
3. **Warmth through restraint** — Maroon palette, generous whitespace, human-feeling typography
4. **Varied rhythm** — Break the monotony of repeated card grids with different layouts per section
5. **Mobile-native** — Design for the phone first, enhance for desktop
